Why roofs fail up here

A roof in Buxton is not a roof in Bolton.

At 1,000 ft, Buxton is one of the highest market towns in England. What passes as a fine specification in the valleys gets taken apart by a Peak District winter. Four things drive nearly every failure we get called to.

Exposure

Severe wind-driven rain

Most of the High Peak sits in BS 5534 exposure zone 4–5. That changes headlap, batten gauge and fixing spec — every slate mechanically fixed, not nail-and-hope. Get it wrong and rain tracks uphill under the covering.

Freeze–thaw

Water that expands inside the tile

We get 60–80 frost days a year. Porous concrete tiles and soft bricks take on water, freeze, and delaminate from the face. It is the single most common reason a 30-year roof only lasts 18 up here.

Movement

Old gritstone walls that shift

Rubble-filled stone walls and softwood structures move. Rigid mortar bedding cracks, ridges lift, valleys open up. We dry-fix ridges and hips as standard and re-point in a lime mortar that flexes with the building.

Planning

National Park & conservation rules

Inside the Peak District National Park and its conservation areas, replacement usually has to be like-for-like — reclaimed stone slate or natural slate, matched coursing, matched ridge. We source it and handle the specification.

What we do

Everything above the wall plate — and a fair bit below it.

We are a full roofing contractor. Complete re-roofs, flat roofing systems, leadwork, roofline, chimneys and structural alterations, all carried out by our own directly employed tradesmen — and yes, we still do repairs, properly. We came up through the heritage side, stone slate and lead, and we are fully qualified on the modern systems too.

Full re-roofs

Strip, structural check, breathable membrane, new battens and covering. Scaffold and skips arranged as part of the job; building control sign-off handled on request.

Flat roofing

Warm and cold deck build-ups with the falls designed in, not chased afterwards. Insulation upgraded to current Part L on every re-cover.

Leadwork

Our own lead welders. Chimney flashings, valleys, box gutters, bay tops and full lead flat roofs, all to Lead Sheet Association detail.

Chimneys

Rebuilds, re-leads, new pots and cowls, and full removal with structural make-good where a stack is redundant.

Roofline

Fascia, soffit, guttering and dry verge in UPVC or timber, with the ventilation actually calculated for the roof void rather than guessed at.

Loft & structural

Dormers, loft conversions, Velux installation and loft insulation upgrades, taken from drawings through to building control.

Re-pointing & masonry protection

Raked out properly and re-pointed to whatever joint the building wants — weather-struck, flush, bucket-handle or recessed — in sand and cement, or a breathable lime mortar on older stone and anything listed. Chimney stacks, gables, parapets, full elevations, ridge and verge bedding. Where a wall is taking driving rain we finish with masonry cream: it makes the face water-repellent without sealing the wall up, so the stone can still breathe.
